Built like a system, not a toy.

Semester was built for real school weeks: flaky WLAN, double lessons, plans that change at 7:45. The boring parts are the feature.

Auto-save on every interaction

Forms have no save button. Every edit commits as you type and closing a form can never lose input, on web and Android alike.

Offline-first sync

Edits live locally first and win over the cloud until their push lands. Structured rows with deterministic ids mean web and phone agree without coordination.

Credentials stay put

Portal logins never leave your device except to your own server for the fetch. Sign out and your data stays with you.

Set up your desk in one afternoon.

Paste your timetable as JSON, connect your school portal, install the app. Everything else follows.
